WindPressure Modeling: Key Points and Next Steps

Context and scaling
- Outcome: ln(Base Damage).
- Wind normalization: by Category‑1 baseline (≈ 82.55 kt). A 1.0 change equals a Cat‑1 increment.
- Pressure normalization: by 980 mb.

Orthogonal residualization result
- Constructed: wind_residual = wind_obs − E[wind|modelled_wind] via linear regression.
- Diagnostic: corr(modelled_wind, wind_residual) ≈ 2.4e−16 → orthogonality achieved.
- Plot shows centered residuals with no clear curvature → residualization behaved as intended.

Coefficient interpretation (example from recent run)
- modelled_wind_coef ≈ 5.15 (sd ≈ 0.42): strong, precise positive effect.
  - Damage multiplier for Δ wind: exp(5.15  Δ). Example: Δ = 0.25   3.57 damage.
- wind_residual_coef  0.35 (sd  1.01): wide, includes 0  little stable signal beyond physics-based wind.

Implications
- Physics-first story: calibrated pressurewind mapping captures most damage-relevant intensity; extra wind deviations contribute little after controlling for the physics term.
- Practical modeling: keep modelled_wind as the primary driver; consider dropping wind_residual unless diagnostics show structure with damage.

When wind-only is sufficient
- For pure prediction with reliable wind: wind_obs alone is often enough; adding raw pressure tends to add collinearity without improving out-of-sample fit.
- Keep a wind-only baseline spec for comparison (use WAIC/LOO).

When pressure still adds value
- Interpretability and causal story (barometric mechanism).
- Missing or biased wind observations.
- Robustness via latent intensity: treat wind and pressure as measurements of a shared latent S.

Decoupling options (interpretable alternatives to PCA)
- Physics + deviation: modelled_wind + wind_residual (current approach; orthogonal by construction).
- Wind-centric: wind_obs + pressure_resid, where pressure_resid = pressure  E[pressure|wind_obs] (e.g., LOWESS).
- Latent intensity: introduce S, use measurement models for wind and pressure; damage depends on S.
- Regularization: group horseshoe or stronger Normal priors if keeping both raw wind and pressure.

Nonlinearity considerations
- If damage response is convex in wind, add modelled_wind^2 or a spline on wind in the damage equation.
- If wind_obs vs modelled_wind shows curvature, residualize using quadratic or LOWESS E[wind|modelled_wind].

Diagnostics checklist
- corr(modelled_wind, wind_residual)  0 (met).
- Residuals vs modelled_wind: no trend/curvature (met visually).
- Residuals vs ln(damage): no structure  consider dropping residual term.
- Coefficient pair plots: severe anti-correlation suggests decorrelation or stronger shrinkage.
- WAIC/LOO and Pareto-k: prefer specs with better out-of-sample performance.

Recommended specs to compare
- Wind-only baseline: {economic, tides, wind_obs, travel_speed, trend}
- Physics-first: {economic, tides, modelled_wind, travel_speed, trend}
- Optional wind-centric residual: add pressure_resid to wind_obs spec
- Optional nonlinearity: add modelled_wind^2 to the physics-first spec

Interpretation formula
- Percent change from wind Δ (normalized): 100  (exp(coef  Δ)  1).
